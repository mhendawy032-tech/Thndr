Add a Buy or Sell Trade

Commission on a buy is added to your cost basis; commission on a sell is deducted from your proceeds — both affect profitability.
Sell orders are blocked if the quantity exceeds what you currently hold for that stock — the trade will not be saved and you'll see an error instead.

Open Positions (still held)

Only stocks you still hold appear here — profits from past sales live in Closed Trades and never affect these numbers. Type a new price on any card to log it to that stock's history. Verdicts (Accumulate / Hold / Watch / Reduce) combine the stock's own trend, how it performs versus EGX30, the overall market direction, concentration and price freshness (EGX trades Sunday–Thursday). These are rule-based guides, not financial advice.
