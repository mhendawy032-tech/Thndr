Add a Buy or Sell Trade

Commission on a buy is added to your cost basis; commission on a sell is deducted from your proceeds — both affect profitability.
Sell orders are blocked if the quantity exceeds what you currently hold for that stock — the trade will not be saved and you'll see an error instead.

Current Portfolio

Stock Current Qty Avg. Buy Price Current Price Stop Loss (−5%) Target 1 (+3%) Target 2 (+6%) Current Value Unrealized P/L Realized P/L Recommendation

Enter your Current Price whenever you check the market — Unrealized P/L is always calculated from that latest value, and the timestamp under it shows when it was last updated. Stop Loss / Target 1 / Target 2 are calculated automatically from your average buy price (−5% / +3% / +6%) and can't be edited manually. Recommendation flags "Sell — Stop Loss hit", "Sell — Target 1 reached" or "Sell — Target 2 reached" once the current price crosses one of those levels, otherwise it shows "Hold" — each alongside your overall gain/loss as a % of your average buy price (▲ for a gain, ▼ for a loss; Realized P/L is used instead once a stock is fully sold). Click the Unrealized P/L or Realized P/L label above to filter this table to just those stocks. Fully sold (closed) positions are highlighted green with their Current Price field locked, and their Recommendation badge uses a distinct color from "Hold ▲/▼" so closed positions stand out.
